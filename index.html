Know Your Risk

Understand Your Personal Prostate Cancer Risk

Age, family history, genetics, and race all shape your individual risk, and understanding them helps you make informed decisions about screening and prevention.

Did you know?

African American men and Caribbean men of African descent have nearly twice the risk of developing prostate cancer compared to other populations. Early screening and awareness save lives.
