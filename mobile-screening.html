The Program

Screening Where People Already Are

Our mobile screening program brings advanced imaging equipment and specialized clinical staff directly into underserved communities, removing the barriers of distance, cost, and access that keep men from getting screened early.

Each unit is equipped to perform a full range of prostate health screenings and follow-up coordination on site — no referral or appointment required.

The program was created to address the disproportionately high incidence of prostate cancer in Black communities — Black men are up to 70% more likely to develop high-risk prostate cancer, and more than twice as likely to die of the disease, compared to white men.

On-Site Services

Services We Provide

Every mobile unit offers a comprehensive set of screening and diagnostic services, with coordinated follow-up for further testing when needed.

PSA Test

A blood test that measures prostate-specific antigen protein levels.

Digital Rectal Exam

Used to check for signs of a prostate problem or prostate cancer.

Micro-Ultrasound Imaging

Visualizes areas of suspected cancer and helps estimate aggressiveness.

Bladder Scanning

Ultrasound-based assessment of bladder volume.

Genomic Testing

Molecular analysis to inform risk and treatment decisions.

Blood Pressure Monitoring

Proactive assessments as part of overall health management.

Uroflowmetry

Accurate assessment of urinary flow for optimal urological health.

Cystoscopy

State-of-the-art diagnostics for urological interventions.

Our teams also coordinate follow-up urine testing and MRI scans and offer pelvic floor rehabilitation support.
