About Million Strong Men

An initiative of the Global Prostate Cancer Research Foundation, committed to advancing prostate cancer science and patient care worldwide.

Our Story

A Global Initiative for Prostate Cancer Awareness & Care

Million Strong Men is a global initiative dedicated to improving prostate cancer awareness, early detection, research, and treatment outcomes. Through community outreach, medical innovation, and international collaboration, we aim to reach one million men worldwide with life-saving education and care.

Our work brings together physicians, researchers, community leaders, and patients to transform prostate cancer care and reduce disparities across populations.

The Situation

Why This Work Matters

Prostate cancer remains the second most common cancer among African American men, after skin cancer. About 1 in 8 men will develop prostate cancer in their lifetime, and about 1 in 40 will die of it.

Limited Healthcare Access

In underserved communities, distance and cost keep men from getting screened.

Delayed Diagnosis

Clinically significant prostate cancer is too often caught late.

Lack of Insurance Coverage

Socioeconomically disadvantaged patients face the greatest barriers to care.

Disease Stigma

Perceived impact on masculinity keeps many men from seeking screening.

Leadership

Led by Dr. Ashutosh "Ash" Tewari

Chairman of Urology at Mount Sinai Health System and one of the world's leading robotic prostate cancer surgeons.

With more than 10,000 robotic prostate surgeries performed, Dr. Tewari has pioneered innovative surgical techniques designed to improve cancer control while preserving quality of life.

Our Goals

What We're Working Toward

Expand Awareness

Expand prostate cancer awareness globally through education and outreach.

Advance Research

Advance research in genomics, imaging, and precision medicine.

Improve Outcomes

Improve treatment outcomes through continued innovation.

Reduce Disparities

Reduce disparities in prostate cancer diagnosis and care across populations.

Million Strong Men is part of a broader effort toward global prostate cancer initiatives — connecting outreach, research, and care programs across regions to close gaps in diagnosis and treatment worldwide.
