Precision Urology™ & Surgical Innovation

An approach that integrates clinical data, genomics, molecular biology, and advanced imaging to deliver personalized diagnosis and treatment.

The Approach

What is Precision Urology™?

Dr. Tewari's work focuses on Precision Urology™, integrating multiple sources of information to deliver personalized prostate cancer diagnosis and treatment tailored to each patient.

Clinical Data

A full picture of the patient's health history and presentation.

Genomic Information

Genetic markers that inform cancer behavior and risk.

Molecular Biology

Cellular-level insight into tumor characteristics.

Advanced Imaging

Precise visualization to guide diagnosis and treatment planning.

Key Innovations

Pioneering Surgical Techniques

These innovations help patients maintain quality of life while receiving effective cancer treatment.

01

Advanced Robotic Technique (ART™)

A surgical approach designed to maximize cancer control while minimizing complications.

02

Nerve-Sparing Techniques

Innovative surgical methods that help preserve erectile function and urinary control after surgery.

03

Total Reconstruction Method

Restores urinary support structures after prostate removal to accelerate continence recovery.

04

Catheter-Free Robotic Prostatectomy

A novel technique designed to reduce discomfort and improve recovery.
