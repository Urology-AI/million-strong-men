Precision Urology™ & Surgical Innovation

An approach that integrates clinical data, genomics, molecular biology, and advanced imaging to deliver personalized diagnosis and treatment.

The Approach

What is Precision Urology™?

Dr. Tewari's work focuses on Precision Urology™, integrating multiple sources of information to deliver personalized prostate cancer diagnosis and treatment tailored to each patient.

Clinical Data

A full picture of the patient's health history and presentation.

Genomic Information

Genetic markers that inform cancer behavior and risk.

Molecular Biology

Cellular-level insight into tumor characteristics.

Advanced Imaging

Precise visualization to guide diagnosis and treatment planning.

Key Innovations

Pioneering Surgical Techniques

These innovations help patients maintain quality of life while receiving effective cancer treatment.

01

Advanced Robotic Technique (ART™)

A surgical approach designed to maximize cancer control while minimizing complications.

02

Nerve-Sparing Techniques

Innovative surgical methods that help preserve erectile function and urinary control after surgery.

03

Total Reconstruction Method

Restores urinary support structures after prostate removal to accelerate continence recovery.

04

Catheter-Free Robotic Prostatectomy

A novel technique designed to reduce discomfort and improve recovery.

AI & Precision Surgery

Uniting Human Surgical Mastery with Machine Intelligence

As a world-renowned urologic oncologist, robotic surgeon, and pioneering leader in the application of artificial intelligence to cancer care, Dr. Tewari is the go-to name in this arena.

Surgical Intelligence

AI-Driven Planning, In the Operating Room

His surgical innovations are tightly integrated with advanced AI-driven planning, intra-operative intelligence, and functional outcome modeling, enabling highly personalized cancer surgery.

Dr. Tewari is an early and leading architect of AI across the full prostate cancer continuum — from population-level screening and risk stratification, to surgical decision-making, computer vision–guided robotics, and longitudinal recovery prediction.
Capabilities

Where AI Meets Surgical Practice

🛡️

NIH-Funded Clinical Safety AI

Federally funded research developing AI systems to improve clinical safety in cancer surgery.

🧮

Multimodal Surgical Risk Calculators

Models that combine clinical, imaging, and genomic data to quantify surgical risk before the operating room.

🧬

Digital-Twin Anatomy Models

Patient-specific 3D models used to plan and simulate surgery ahead of time.

👁️

Computer Vision for Robotic Surgery

Real-time visual intelligence that supports precision during robotic procedures.

🎯

AI-Assisted Nerve-Sparing Optimization

Intelligent guidance that helps preserve nerve function critical to post-surgical quality of life.

🔬

Real-Time Histopathology Intelligence

AI-assisted tissue analysis that speeds and sharpens diagnostic decision-making.

📊

Population-Level Risk Stratification

AI-supported screening tools that identify high-risk patients earlier across entire populations.

Longitudinal Recovery Prediction

Models that forecast functional recovery over time to guide personalized post-surgical care.
